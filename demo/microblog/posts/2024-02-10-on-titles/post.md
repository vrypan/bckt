When you just want to post a thought or drop a link, picking a title
is unnecessary friction. 

bckt supports a `type: note` front matter field exactly for this — leave
the title out, set the type, and the post renders without one.

It works especially well with microblog themes like this one, which are designed around titleless entries. The date becomes the anchor instead.
